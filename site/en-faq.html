FAQ

Before you join

Do I need prior experience to use the system?+
No, but it's best to start with two or three modules until you master reading them before enabling the rest, to avoid visual clutter.
Does it work on every market?+
Yes, it works on any symbol available on TradingView (stocks, indices, forex, commodities, crypto) and any timeframe.
Does it give automatic recommendations?+
No, the system is an analysis and reading tool that supports your decision — it is not a recommendation or automated execution. The decision and risk remain yours.
How do I get the indicator?+
Sign up from the button at the top of the page, and you'll receive the access link and instructions to add the indicator on TradingView.
Are future updates available?+
Yes, the system is continuously developed, and updates plus any new modules reach you automatically.
Do I need a paid TradingView plan?+
You only need a plan that supports invite-only scripts, which is available even on TradingView's basic paid plans.
Can I use it on more than one device?+
Yes, access is tied to your TradingView account, so you can open it from any device you're signed in on.
Does it work on the mobile app?+
Yes, it works on the TradingView mobile app exactly as it does on the web version.
Are there alerts?+
Yes, nearly every module carries independent alert conditions you can enable from TradingView's "Create Alert" menu.
Does the indicator slow down the chart?+
No, the modules are built within calculated drawing limits (lines, labels, tables) to stay under TradingView's maximum drawing cap.
Can I change the colors and settings?+
Yes, nearly every color and setting is adjustable from the indicator's settings menu to match your taste and reading style.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes, the subscription is flexible with no long-term commitment — pause or renew whenever you like.
Do you support new traders?+
Yes, the educational academy on the site explains every part of the three tools step by step for beginners.
Does it work on futures and forex?+
Yes, as long as the symbol is available on TradingView with sufficient price and volume data, the indicator works on it without exception.
Are the signals non-repainting?+
Most modules are built on a confirmed candle close before issuing any signal or alert, and this is stated explicitly in every module that relies on it (such as the Shield module).
Can I share my subscription with a friend?+
Access is personal and tied to your own TradingView account only, and may not be shared with a third party.
Is my data and trading history safe?+
The indicator runs entirely inside your own TradingView account, and we never access your portfolio, trades, or any personal data.
What's the difference between the three tools?+
O P N LIO reads trend and momentum, Smart Pivot maps support/resistance, and Empire of the Wealthy reads liquidity and hidden confluence — see the "System" page above.
Can you arrange live one-on-one training?+
Message us to discuss details — availability depends on current demand.
Does the free trial require a bank card?+
No, the 14-day free trial starts immediately with no payment details required.
What do I do if I hit a technical issue?+
Reach out to us directly and we'll help as soon as possible.
