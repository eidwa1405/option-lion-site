Questions before you pay

How do I cancel?+
From your member dashboard, hit cancel — auto-renewal stops instantly and your indicator access keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. No call, no reason needed.
What do I actually pay after tax?+
Prices here are before tax. VAT is added automatically at checkout according to your country, and the final amount is shown before you confirm.
When are the indicators activated on my account?+
Access is granted to the TradingView username you enter at checkout, and the tools appear under Invite-only scripts. If it is delayed, message us and it is sorted in minutes.
Is the academy part of the subscription?+
The Indicator System track is free for everyone, no subscription. The Trader Mastery track has a token one-time registration fee of $1. The Smart Session Clock track opens when you buy the Session Clock — indicator and track together for one price.
Is the Session Clock included in the indicators subscription?+
No. The Session Clock is a standalone one-time purchase covering both the TradingView indicator and its ten academy levels for one price. The track opens only after purchase.
Do the indicators give recommendations?+
No. The system is a set of market-reading tools showing probabilities and technical conditions; the decision and its responsibility are yours alone. The content is educational and analytical, not investment advice.
