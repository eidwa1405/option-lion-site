CASE STUDY — US500

Monthly negative reversal, then confirmed on the daily after a failed attempt

On the monthly timeframe a negative reversal candle appeared synced with a buying-side liquidity surge and a negative divergence — and the daily timeframe echoed the same negative reversal, an early warning of a downward move. On the daily chart a first positive reversal appeared but price kept declining the next day (a failed early attempt), then a second positive reversal appeared and was actually confirmed the following day — illustrating the importance of waiting for confirmation rather than rushing the first signal.
